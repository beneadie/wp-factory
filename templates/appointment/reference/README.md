# Appointment — reference captures

Three screenshots are enough to judge this template. Capture at 1× device scale with the shared fonts loaded; do not crop the page background, since the warm ground against white surfaces is part of the design.

## 1. `01-hero-1440.png`

Home page at 1440px, viewport from the top through the stat band.

Must demonstrate:

- the 6/4 split with the display headline at its full 72px size, the lede at a comfortable measure, one dark primary button and the quiet "or call" alternative;
- the bordered white "Next available" card as the only visual object: 1px frame, internal rule, tabular date, two 48px time chips, one accent button, "See the full week" link;
- generous 88px band spacing, no shadow under the card, and the white stat band's hairline dividers.

## 2. `02-timetable-1440.png`

The treatments and timetable section at 1440px, scrolled so the table has moved inside its own panel and a day header is pinned at the top.

Must demonstrate:

- the two-column treatment ledger: display-face names, muted durations, fees aligned in one right-hand column with tabular numerals;
- the sticky day header sitting flush at the top of the timetable panel while rows pass beneath it;
- 1px separators only, times in tabular numerals, treatment names in Petrona, and the filled "Book" pill next to the outlined "Full" pill;
- the legend and the "confirmed by text message" note, with no icon clutter and no horizontal scrollbar.

## 3. `03-page-head-390.png`

A thin page (for example `/treatments/`) at 390px, top of the page through the three links.

Must demonstrate:

- the composed PageHead with no image and no body copy: eyebrow, display h1, one hairline, three numbered sibling links;
- the links stacking cleanly with hairline separators and no cramped tap targets;
- the display face at its 44px mobile size with no clipping, and the same 20px page gutter used by the hero;
- that the template still feels intentional at phone width, with air above and below the title.

## Capture checks (all three)

- No shadow anywhere; structure is hairlines and whitespace.
- No display italic, no script face, no third typeface.
- Every price, time and stat uses tabular numerals.
- The page ground, white surfaces and dark inverse band are the only large colour fields; accent appears as the card's one solid button, its dot and the short rules. Filled timetable pills use ink, not accent.
